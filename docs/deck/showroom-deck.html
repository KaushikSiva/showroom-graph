05 / Customer & business opportunity

Start with one room.
Earn the next decision.

A focused entry point: renters and homeowners planning a living-room refresh, with an eventual workspace for independent interior designers.

The customer

Someone with a real room, a fixed budget and a fear of buying the wrong piece.

The business

Test paid design briefs and a designer subscription. Retail referrals are an optional, disclosed model.

The next proof

Run ten guided room sessions. Measure time to a saved brief, budget fit and willingness to pay.

Business hypotheses, not traction claims. No revenue, customer counts, hardware benchmarks or award outcomes are asserted.
